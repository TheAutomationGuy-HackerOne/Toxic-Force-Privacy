Privacy Policy for Toxic Force Magic Calculator

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Overview

Toxic Force Magic Calculator (“the App”) is a free calculator for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes how the App Store build handles information.

Short version: free to use, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Calculations and any settings stay on your device. Nothing is sold or shared.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the iOS app published as Toxic Force Magic Calculator (App Store name). It does not cover third-party websites you might open from a link in the App (for example a support page).

Information we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or other contact details
  • Accounts, sign-in, or passwords
  • Location (GPS)
  • Advertising identifiers or IDFA
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking
  • Crash reporters that send personal data off-device
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera
  • Health, fitness, or financial data

There is no advertising SDK, no analytics package, and no third-party tracker in the App. The App is free and does not show ads.

What stays on your device

The App runs on your device. Any preferences or in-session calculator state stay local so the App can work for you next time.

Deleting the App removes everything it has stored on that device.

Network access

The published App does not need network access to calculate. It does not send your calculations, settings, or personal information to our servers.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share personal information with third parties. There is no account system, so there is no profile to share.

Third-party services

The App does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, advertising networks, social pixels, or similar trackers.

Apple provides the App Store and device security. Those are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

A “Buy me a coffee” or similar optional support link in the App, if present and you tap it, opens a third-party site in the browser. That site has its own policy. Tapping it is optional.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can clear or change any in-app preferences inside the App.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove local data.

We cannot retrieve your settings because they live on your device, not on an account of ours.

California (CCPA) and Europe (GDPR)

We do not sell personal information. We do not run an advertising or analytics profile. Calculator use happens on your device. If you have a privacy request, use the contact path below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top will change and the new text will be posted at this URL. The App Store listing points here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project at github.com/TheAutomationGuy-HackerOne/Toxic-Force/issues.
